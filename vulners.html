Challenge

Vulners is a cybersecurity startup with multiple products—Database, Scanner, Feed, Notifications, API, and AI Scoring—competing with companies like Snyk, Feedly, and Debricked in a crowded market of similar offerings. Each product had different endpoints and narratives, when they made more sense as part of a unified suite.

The founders identified a critical problem through direct user feedback: the fragmented brand was costing them revenue and failing to attract target customers. Decision-makers—often managers without deep technical expertise—struggled to understand what made Vulners valuable.

My Role

I was brought in as a contractor for 2 months, reporting directly to the CTO on a team of fewer than 10 people. I contributed to every aspect: research, brand identity, design system, and implementation across landing pages, product UI, and data visualization.

I conducted research with founders and users to understand their experience, motivations, and what could increase interest in Vulners. Based on business metrics and strategic goals, I identified that a design system would provide consistency between landing pages and the web-based product, while enabling scalability beyond the initial implementations.

The timeline was aggressive—research, brand adjustments, design system foundations, and implementations all within 2 months. I worked directly with the CTO, balancing speed with quality handoffs for the small team.

Solution

Design System Foundations

Created comprehensive system covering:

  • Color tokens and typography for brand consistency
  • Component library with over 40 reusable elements, cutting down development time from custom builds
  • Data visualization patterns tailored for security analytics
  • Layout systems designed for landing pages and products

Approachable Visual Language

Developed an illustration style that:

  • Made complex security concepts approachable for managers
  • Maintained technical credibility for security engineers
  • Unified the Vulners ecosystem visually
  • Scaled from abstract concepts to specific tools

Illustration system balancing approachability (for managers) with technical credibility (for security engineers).

Implementation

Applied the system across:

  • Landing pages: Improved conversion narrative
  • Data visualization: Consistent, readable security analytics
  • Marketing materials: Clear product positioning
  • Product foundations: Component library for future development

Data visualization and landing pages shipped during my engagement. Product UI foundations were handed off for continued development.

Impact

20% improvement in signups

10% increase in conversions

Foundations Delivered

Handed off comprehensive deliverables: research reports, design system, and visual identity—all in Figma. The team built on these foundations after I moved to another role.

Long-term Adoption

The original design system building blocks are still in use, though Vulners has evolved the aesthetic as they've grown. Data visualization and landing pages shipped as designed.

Key Learnings

Technical depth is rewarding

Diving into cybersecurity concepts was challenging but rewarding. Understanding the technical domain deeply enough to explain it simply made the visual direction more effective.

Small teams need clean handoffs

For teams under 10 people, less is more. Assets need to be easy to use and well-documented. Clean handoff matters more than perfect execution when you won't be there for implementation.

Balance approachability with credibility

Cybersecurity audiences range from technical engineers to non-technical managers. The visual direction needed to appeal to decision-makers without alienating the experts who would actually use the product.
